Modular House Steel Frame Structure Design Explained

The backbone of any high-end modular building is its steel frame structure. Utilizing galvanized structural steel and precision welding technology, modern prefab units achieve earthquake resistance, wind load tolerance, and decades of structural integrity.

1. Galvanized Steel Framework and Anti-Corrosion Treatment

Primary steel components undergo hot-dip galvanizing and multi-layer protective coating to prevent rust, even in humid coastal environments.

2. Composite Insulation Wall Panels

Sandwich wall panels featuring EPS, XPS, or PIR insulation cores deliver superior thermal efficiency, reducing heating and cooling energy consumption.
