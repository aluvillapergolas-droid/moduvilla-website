Frequently Asked Questions — Aluminum Pergolas

1. Can AluVilla pergolas be paired with ModuVilla modular houses or CoveCore outdoor kitchens?
Yes. AluVilla pergolas are engineered to integrate seamlessly with ModuVilla prefab villas, CoveCore 304 stainless steel outdoor kitchens, and container pools, allowing consolidated container shipping from China.
2. How does the integrated rainwater drainage system work?
When the roof louvers close, interlocking channels direct rainwater into perimeter U-shaped gutters concealed inside the horizontal beams, draining quietly down through the hollow vertical posts to ground level.
3. Do you offer custom dimensions, RAL colors, and private-label OEM?
Yes. Beyond standard 3×3m to 4×8m sizes, we fabricate custom dimensions up to 7m×6m without center posts, offer custom architectural RAL powder coatings, and provide OEM branding for distributors.
4. What is the warranty, MOQ, and lead time?
We provide a 5-year structural frame warranty and 2-year motor/electronics warranty. Standard MOQ is 4 sets (mixable models/sizes) with a 20–35 day production lead time and full pre-shipment inspection videos.
