Factory Capabilities & Frequently Asked Questions

Global Manufacturing Bases, OEM/ODM Policies & Quality Assurance

1. OEM & ODM Custom Manufacturing

We accept both OEM and ODM orders. For private-label brand manufacturing (OEM), clients are required to provide valid trademark registration certificates and brand authorization documentation.

2. Global Production Bases & Product Matrix

We operate specialized manufacturing facilities across Hengshui (Hebei), Kuala Lumpur (Malaysia), and Foshan (Guangdong) for outdoor pergolas, alongside modular housing, mobile sanitary units, outdoor kitchens, container pools, and partner lines.

1. How are the products installed and used? Are specific instructions provided?
Every order comes with detailed step-by-step installation manuals, user operation guides, and dedicated remote technical support from our engineering team.
2. What after-sales warranty and support do you offer?
We provide a 1-year product warranty. For non-product or operational issues beyond the warranty scope, we continue to offer ongoing technical consultation and support.
3. What is the typical production lead time?
Standard production & delivery lead time is 20–35 days depending on order quantity. Any force majeure or special circumstances will be communicated in advance, while custom OEM/ODM designs require confirming a specific production schedule.
4. How do you ensure consistent product quality?
We enforce full-process quality control from raw materials to assembly, provide finished-product packaging and inspection videos before shipment, and fully support third-party quality inspections.
