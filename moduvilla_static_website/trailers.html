Factory Capabilities & Frequently Asked Questions

Dezhou Manufacturing Base, OEM/ODM Policies & Export Compliance

1. OEM & ODM Custom Trailer Manufacturing

We accept both OEM and ODM orders for global trailer dealerships. We support custom chassis dimensions, GVM ratings, paint/galvanizing finishes, World Manufacturer Identifier (WMI/VIN) stamping, and private-label branding.

2. Manufacturing Base & Product Matrix

Located in Linyi County, Dezhou City, Shandong Province, our modern facility produces 16 core models across Flatbed Cargo Trailers, Car Haulers, Fenced Utility Trailers, and 2–4 Horse Floats built to ADR, DOT, and CE standards.

1. How are the trailers shipped and assembled? Are instructions provided?
To maximize 40HQ container loading capacity and reduce ocean freight costs, trailers are shipped in CKD/SKD (semi-knocked-down) racks. Every shipment includes detailed step-by-step assembly manuals, wiring diagrams, and remote video guidance from our engineers.
2. What structural warranty and after-sales support do you offer?
We provide a 2-year structural chassis warranty and 1-year warranty on axles, brakes, and electrical components. Replacement parts can be dispatched worldwide via express air or bundled with your next container order.
3. What is the typical production lead time?
Standard production & delivery lead time is 20–35 days depending on order quantity and container configuration. Custom gooseneck horse floats or specialized OEM designs will be confirmed with an exact production schedule prior to deposit.
4. How do you ensure consistent welding and anti-rust quality?
We utilize robotic CO2/MIG welding jigs, full hot-dip galvanizing baths (zinc coating ≥85μm), and ADR-certified axles/couplers. Pre-shipment inspection videos and load-testing reports are provided before container sealing.
